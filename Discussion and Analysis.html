Discussion and Analysis of Information Found

After analysis of the questionnaire responses, the following information has been extracted:

Questionnaire Findings

The questionnaire responses show that online gaming is a common activity among the respondents, although the amount of time spent gaming varies from person to person. The survey found that most respondents spend less than one hour per day gaming during school holidays. Fewer respondents spend between one and six hours gaming, while only a very small number spend more than six hours. This suggests that the majority of respondents participate in online gaming in moderation rather than excessively.

Negative Effects of Online Gaming

The questionnaire also explored the negative effects associated with online gaming. The results showed that 12 out of 25 respondents reported experiencing no negative effects, making this the most common response. However, several respondents reported experiencing negative consequences. The most frequently reported problems were reduced physical activity and lack of sleep, while only a small number of respondents reported addiction or overuse. These findings suggest that although most participants appear to use online gaming responsibly, excessive gaming can negatively affect a person's health and daily routine.

Supporting Research

Research from reliable educational and health sources supports these findings. Organizations such as the World Health Organization (WHO), Harvard Health Publishing, the American Psychological Association (APA) and Common Sense Media explain that online gaming can provide benefits such as improved problem-solving skills, teamwork, communication and stress relief. However, these sources also warn that excessive gaming may contribute to poor sleeping habits, reduced physical activity, addiction and lower academic performance if it is not properly managed.

Overall Opinions

The overall opinions collected through the questionnaire further support this balanced view of online gaming. Of the 25 respondents:

  • 21 believed that online gaming has both benefits and disadvantages.
  • 3 believed that it is mostly harmful.
  • 1 considered it mostly beneficial.

These results indicate that most participants recognize that online gaming is neither entirely positive nor entirely negative. Instead, its effects depend largely on how much time is spent gaming and whether healthy limits are maintained.

Data Analysis

The spreadsheet calculations, database queries and graphs helped identify these patterns more clearly. Grouping respondents according to age, gender, gaming frequency and opinions made it easier to compare responses and identify the most common behaviors and experiences among participants.

Overall, the collected data supports the information found in the research sources. The findings suggest that moderate gaming is generally associated with positive experiences, while excessive gaming is linked to negative health and lifestyle effects.
