The impact of Online Gaming on consumers

What is this website about?

Online gaming has become one of the most popular forms of entertainment among people of all ages, especially teenagers and young adults. With the increasing availability of the internet, smartphones, computers and gaming consoles, more people are spending time playing online games for entertainment, relaxation and social interaction. Online gaming allows players to connect with friends and other players around the world while developing skills such as teamwork, communication and problem-solving. However, excessive gaming has also raised concerns about its possible effects on academic performance, physical health, mental well-being and social relationships.

The purpose of this investigation was to answer the focus question: "What are the effects, benefits and disadvantages of online gaming?" The investigation aimed to determine how online gaming influences people's daily lives by examining both its positive and negative effects. Information was gathered from reliable websites, journals and other credible sources, and a questionnaire was designed and completed by 25 respondents to collect first-hand data. The responses were captured in Microsoft Excel and Microsoft Access, where functions, formulas, queries, reports and graphs were used to analyses the data. The results were then interpreted to identify trends and draw meaningful conclusions about online gaming and its impact on consumers.
